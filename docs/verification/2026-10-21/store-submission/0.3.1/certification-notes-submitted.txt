No account or sign-in is required. This is a C#/.NET WinForms desktop lighting utility. The runFullTrust capability is required for the desktop process model, Windows WASAPI loopback playback analysis and Windows Dynamic Lighting LampArray control. Audio is processed only in memory. The app does not open the microphone capture endpoint, record files, upload audio or analysis, or include advertising, tracking or telemetry. Only lighting preferences are saved locally.

Without hardware: Open the Lighting Settings, About/Version and Privacy Policy tabs. Play audio through the Windows default multimedia output and select Start Sync. The preview reacts to audio and fades after playback stops. Switch audio basis, palette, animation and speed. Stop sync before changing lamp count. Close and reopen to check saved preferences. Close Application, window X and the tray menu all end the process and release resources.

Physical lighting requires this project's ESP32-S3 USB Light Strip HID LampArray firmware (VID 303A, PID 1001), correctly wired WS2812 LEDs, and an app lamp count matching the firmware. Enable the device in Windows Dynamic Lighting and assign appropriate background lighting priority to the app. No remote test device is provided. The physical prototype has 8 LEDs. The configurable 1-64 range does not mean a 64-LED strip or its USB power consumption has been physically verified. A changing preview alone is not proof of physical lighting control.

Windows 11 x64 is required. The package minimum is build 22631 (23H2); the app additionally checks availability of the required LampArray API. Development testing used Windows 11 24H2. The app respects Windows lighting arbitration. Privacy policy is available offline in the app and publicly in the project repository.
